## What to emulate (observed technique)

- **Medium/form:** Blender 3D, cel-shaded anime models; an edited 2:17
  scene with cuts between set camera angles (close-up, from above, from
  behind) — not a single loop. Within shots, motion is cyclic (breathing,
  rhythmic rocking); the structure across shots is phased: activity →
  climax → after-phase with continued stimulation.
- **Planted contact:** hands placed on the partner's head/leg/thigh (or own
  chest) *stay planted* — they move with the body, no floating, no sliding,
  no clipping apart between beats. This is the reference's headline quality
  and Handfast's core test.
- **Breathing as a base layer:** continuous chest rise/fall under all other
  motion, tagged and visible throughout.
- **Secondary motion:** long hair, sidelocks/braid, and ears lag the head/
  torso with a slight delay — never rigidly attached.
- **Expression continuity:** eyes/mouth evolve through intermediate
  positions (half-lidded, gradual opening) rather than snapping between
  fixed states; face supports the breathing and the vocal rhythm.
- **Voice as timing:** a credited voice performance is part of the scene's
  clock (compare Redlight's reactive-voice law, W5).
