# Adapt — the partnered build-up (fathom RE 8245833) for Handfast

Design only; no game code changes, no new character generation
(D-292). Source: the fathom RE of Danbooru post 8245833
(`~/workspace/fathom-re-buildup-20261031/RECIPE.md`) — a 98.7 s
toy-play build-up measured as a staircase of held intensity
floors (×1.0 → ×17.0) with one dipping plateau and a single
sustained peak. Register for this game, per the commission:
**partnered contact** — in the source the build is solo and
machine-held; Handfast's whole subject is the two-body beat, so
the adaptation's first and largest move is stated up front:
**the apparatus's job is given to Prima's hands.** Every hold
the machine performs in the source becomes a planted-hand
contact in Handfast's grammar, and the escalation the machine
schedules becomes an escalation Prima conducts.

## What carries over

- **The staircase, conducted by hand.** The build runs as one
  long beat in Handfast's grammar — `approach → contact →
  guidance → held appraisal` stretched to sitting length: each
  of the source's eight segments is a *guidance stage* whose
  intensity floor (×1.0 / 2.1 / 3.8 / 5.7 / 8.8 / 7.7 / 17.0 /
  12.6, from the recipe) is set by Prima's working hand and
  held, not ramped. Floors step at stage boundaries, where the
  guiding hand visibly re-seats — the re-seat is the cut, in
  rig terms: a contact broken and re-made at a new anchor,
  never a slide.
- **Contact is stored as (figure, anchor), as always.** The
  working hand is a contact record on Sloane — `(Sloane,
  anchor)` with the anchor migrating between stages (thigh →
  hip → device hand → shoulder, per the source's pose
  openings) — and its world position is derived every tick by
  the `carry_hand()` rule, so the hand is carried by Sloane's
  breathing and sway exactly as the planted hand of 5447477
  was. The device itself is a second contact: Prima's other
  hand on the device is `(Sloane, device)` from stage 2 on,
  which is what makes the build *operated* rather than merely
  witnessed.
- **The ≥95% acceptance metric applies per stage.** Each stage
  is an intimate shot for the metric's purposes: contact in the
  active zone for ≥95% of its frames, with the stage-boundary
  re-seats excluded by the same accounting the v1.0 fuzz test
  uses (the hand within tear distance of its anchor-or-path for
  20 000 random-input steps). The peak stage is held to the
  full metric — it is where a lesser build would let the hand
  drift, and the source's own contact zone never empties
  either.
- **The plateau is Prima's held breath.** Stage 6 (0.88× of
  sustain) is conducted as a deliberate easing of the working
  hand while the *other* channels keep climbing — Sloane's
  face ladder and the dual-gaze channel carry the rise the
  hand sets down. In a two-presiding-styles room this is
  Prima's induction register doing the plateau: less pressure,
  more presence, and Sloane's tearful near-peak arrives *to
  the gaze*, not to the hand.

## Mechanics mapping (onto the shipped systems)

- **Beat machine:** the eight stages are eight holds in the
  beat state machine, each with its own duration budget (the
  held beat is the longest element — the source's sustain and
  plateau stages are its two longest, 17.0 s and 17.9 s of
  98.7 s, and the adaptation keeps that proportion as stage
  budgets, scaled to the sitting).
- **Expression ramps:** Sloane's face climbs the source ladder
  (composed → absorbed → strained → tearful → climax) one
  state per stage, every channel ramping at bounded rate
  (90 milli per tick class), never snapping at a stage
  boundary. Her activity multiplier stays ×1.30 against
  Prima's — the receiving figure is the more expressive one,
  in this source as in 5447477.
- **Dual gaze:** Prima's gaze stays on Sloane's face through
  the plateau and peak (the induction register's attention);
  Sloane's gaze target migrates — partner → middle distance →
  closed — and returns at the aftermath stage. The iris-glow
  layer follows its standing rule: a scene-state-switched
  layer (beat ∧ regard threshold ∧ setting), never a property
  of either model.
- **Breathing:** the base layer runs under everything, Prima
  ~4.3 s / Sloane ~4.9 s, amplitude scaled ×1.0 / 1.15 / 1.30
  across the sitting's thirds — at the peak stage Sloane's
  breath amplitude is the aftermath's first signal, since the
  source gives no taper for the hand to play.
- **Appraisal:** the Appraisal Brush scores the guided hand in
  this game; here it scores the *conducting* — floor-keeping
  per stage, re-seat cleanliness at boundaries, and the
  plateau's restraint (a stage-6 floor above sustain is a
  scored fault: Prima failed to ease). The held appraisal
  terminal beat reads the whole staircase as one evidence
  record.

## Face states

Per stage, both figures: Sloane climbs the five-state ladder
(two stages composed, two absorbed, sustain strained, plateau
tearful, peak climax, aftermath open-eyed and spent — the
source's aftermath frames keep the face lit, and Handfast
keeps it in frame, unlike the source's final framing). Prima's
face is the register contrast the game exists for: induction
composure throughout, one permitted crack at the peak stage —
a single brow/lid ramp event, scored as presence, not failure.

## Divergences from the source, plainly

- **Solo → duet is the adaptation, not a detail.** The source
  is one figure and a machine; every apparatus function
  (holding the legs, scheduling the floors, keeping the
  device) is reassigned to a partner's two hands and one
  presiding style. Nothing of the machine enters the game.
- The source's camera drops the face at the peak; Handfast
  holds the face — its acceptance metric and its whole beat
  grammar are face-led (the 5447477 lesson), so the peak is
  read on both faces.
- The source has no daze and no taper; the aftermath stage
  ends the sitting elevated, and the held appraisal — not a
  cool-down — closes the beat.
- The viewfinder overlay and the room are the source's
  production design; neither ports.
