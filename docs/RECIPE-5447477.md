## 4. The reconstructed production recipe (derived)

1. **Models.** Two cel-shaded 3D anime figures: toon shading with soft
   gradients, a glowing-iris eye material. One environment set: rocks,
   oversized red foliage, water, floating light motes, magenta twilight
   grade (palette above).
2. **Rigs.** Full-body skeletal rigs; facial controls continuous
   (eyes/mouth/blush as blendshape weights, not swaps); hair, sidelocks,
   braid, and ears on lagging chains (bones or physics) driven by head
   and torso motion.
3. **Animation method.** Keyframed pose-to-pose, interpolated on every
   frame. **Breathing is a perpetual additive layer** (chest/shoulders)
   under all other motion. Primary action cycles run **0.5–0.67 s**,
   humanised: intensity ramps, and the cycle is *interrupted* for
   reaction and repositioning beats instead of looping mechanically.
4. **Contact method — the headline technique.** Hands are planted with
   IK-style targets parented to the *partner's* body, so a planted hand
   is carried by the partner's motion: no sliding, no floating, no
   clipping apart. Contact persists across entire shots (97% zone
   activity in the held shot) and is only broken by cuts.
5. **Camera and edit.** ≈ 24 shots from a small set of set angles
   (face close-up, from above, from behind, medium two-shot), one
   framing per ≈ 5 s, slow and restrained intra-shot camera, cuts on
   phase beats — and the discipline to hold the final shot for 19.4 s.
6. **Structure.** Four phases (§2); climax placed in the **72–86%**
   band; the after-phase decelerates into a face-led exchange — the
   emotional beat lands *after* the act, at the longest hold.
7. **Sound.** A credited voice performance tracked to the action
   (≈ 60 events/min in active spans, median onset gap 0.8 s), body/foley
   impacts under it, and **deliberate silence** (21 gaps) as a rhythmic
   device. Voice is part of the timing, not decoration over it.

## 5. Building it as Handfast (W4, Rust — the jam translation)

Handfast ships one two-body beat in the jam's 2D/wasm form; the recipe
translates law-for-law into its mechanism group:

- **Two-body staging / occlusion** ← §4.1–4.2: two rigged figures on the
  Torogao rig semantics; z-order and overlap rules authored per beat.
- **Planted contact** ← §4.4: implement as *coupled anchors* — a hand
  anchor locked to a partner anchor point, carried by the partner's
  motion with secondary lag ≤ 17 ms. Contact attempts in the design
  become attempts to *establish* a planted anchor; success = the anchor
  holds. The reference's bar: once planted, it does not slide.
- **Breathing base layer** ← §4.3: the rig's idle/breath layer is always
  on, in every state, never gated.
- **Guided hand + appraisal** ← §4.4–4.6 at beat scale: structure the
  single beat in the reference's phase discipline — approach → contact
  established → guidance → held appraisal — with the held gaze as the
  beat's "longest shot": restraint instead of coverage.
- **Expression continuity** ← §2: face-state changes interpolate through
  intermediates (Torogao face-canon transitions); no pose snapping, on
  either figure. Sloane (the receiving figure in the beat) carries the
  higher activity, per the measured 2.64 vs 2.08 asymmetry.
- **Cycles** ← §1: the beat's motion cycle sits in the 0.5–0.67 s band
  with scripted interruptions (a reaction, a repositioning) rather than
  a clean loop.
- **Deltas to beat, not match:** the reference renders 30 fps; Handfast's
  bar is 60. The reference is an edited film; Handfast is one held
  framing — its camera law is the final shot's, not the edit's.

## 6. Building it in Torogao main (the 3D form)

This recipe is the lumen pipeline's target form, and it converts into

- **Factory (lumen).** Sprite-to-3D output must carry: toon grade in the

  not scene-level polish.
- **Rig runtime (lumen-rig-core).** The contact system implements §4.4

  four-phase shape; climax lands in the 72–86% band of scene runtime.
- **Arousal engine.** The phase shape maps onto the engine's tiers —
